# Blog

The blog is where everyone on the instance can write about cooking: a weekly menu, a market
haul, a story behind a family dish. Unlike a recipe, a post is free text, with headings,
pictures, links, and **Cocotte recipes embedded in the text** as clickable cards.

> [!NOTE]
> Button labels are quoted in **English**. Switch the language with the flag button if you want
> them to match (see [The interface](index.md#language-and-theme)).

## Read the blog

Click **Blog** in the top bar (or in the tab bar on a phone), or one of the **Latest blog posts**
on the home page. Posts are listed newest first, ten per page, each with its title, author, date,
the first lines of text and its cover image (or, failing that, the first picture of its text). Click a post to read it. You don't need an account
to read posts or to comment on them.

![The blog list, filtered on one author, with the filters on the left](../assets/screenshots/blog-list.png)

### Search and filter

The filters work like the ones of the recipe list (see
[The recipe list](browsing-recipes.md#the-recipe-list)): a column on the left on a computer, a
**Filters** button above the list on a phone.

- **Search** looks for your words in the title, the text and the author's name of each post.
- **Author** shows the posts of one person only. The list offers everyone who has published a
  post.

Each active filter also shows as a pill (for example *By Camille*): click its **×** to remove it,
or click **Reset** to remove them all. Filters are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark
or share a filtered list.

![A blog post with an embedded recipe card and the comments below](../assets/screenshots/blog-post.png)

An embedded recipe shows as a card with its picture, title, total time and servings. Click it
to open the recipe. If the recipe was deleted since, the card says
*This recipe is no longer available.*

Under the post, the **Comments** section works like the one on recipes (see
[Comments](browsing-recipes.md#comments)). If the author turned comments off, existing comments
stay visible and the form is replaced by *Comments are closed for this post.*

## Write a post

*Logged-in users only.*

1. In **Blog**, click **Write a post**.
2. Type a **Title**.
3. Optionally, click **Add a cover image** and pick a picture (see [Cover image](#cover-image)).
4. Write the **Content** in the editor (see below).
5. Untick **Allow comments** if you don't want comments on this post.
6. Read the **Before publishing** rules, tick **My post follows these rules**, and click
   **Publish**.

![The post editor with its toolbar, an embedded recipe and the publishing rules](../assets/screenshots/blog-editor.png)

### Cover image

The cover image is shown across the top of the post and on its card, in the blog list and on the
home page. After picking one, a preview appears with **Change the image** and **Remove**. The
image is uploaded when you click **Publish** (8 MB maximum).

A post without a cover image uses the first picture of its text on its card, and none at the top
of the post.

### The editor

The editor shows the post as it will look once published. Its toolbar offers, from left to
right: **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Heading**, **Subheading**, **Bulleted list**,
**Numbered list**, **Quote**, **Link**, **Insert an image**, **Recipe**, **Undo** and **Redo**.
The usual keyboard shortcuts also work (Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+Z, … or ⌘ on a Mac).

- **Link**: select some text, click the button and type the address. Leave the address empty to
  remove a link.
- **Insert an image**: pick a picture on your device. It is uploaded and inserted where the
  cursor is (8 MB maximum).

### Embed a recipe

1. Put the cursor where the recipe should go.
2. Click **Recipe** in the toolbar, or press **Ctrl+Alt+R** (**⌘ ⌥ R** on a Mac).
3. In **Insert a recipe**, type part of the recipe's name and pick it in the suggestions.

The recipe card appears in the text. Click it once to select it: you can then drag it elsewhere,
or delete it with the Backspace key. Any recipe of the instance can be embedded, not only yours.

### Publishing rules

Every post must follow two rules, recalled above the **Publish** button. Cocotte asks you to
confirm them each time you publish or edit a post:

- no racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic or otherwise discriminatory language, and no hateful
  or harassing content;
- texts and images must respect copyright: only publish your own, or content you are allowed to
  reuse, and credit its author.

Admins can remove posts that break them (see [Moderation](../admin-guide/moderation.md#blog-posts)).

## Edit or delete a post

**Edit** and **Delete** buttons appear on your posts, both in the blog list and under the title of
the post itself (admins see them on every post). A post edited after publication
shows *updated on* with the date next to its publication date. Deleting a post also deletes its
comments, and cannot be undone.

As the author, you can also hide and unhide comments on your posts, exactly like on your
recipes (see [Hide and unhide comments](../admin-guide/moderation.md#hide-and-unhide-comments)).
