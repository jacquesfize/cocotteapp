# My tags

Tags are your own labels for recipes, such as *🎂 Birthdays*, *To try* or *Kids love it*. You can
put them on any recipe you can open, yours or someone else's. **Only you can see them**: other
users never see your tags, and you never see theirs.

Each tag has a name, an optional emoji and a color, and shows up as a colored pill with that
emoji and name.

> [!NOTE]
> Tags need an account. The **My tags** card, the **My tags** filter and the pills on recipe
> cards only appear when you're logged in.

## Tag a recipe

On a recipe page, the **My tags** card sits below the recipe, above **Add to planner**:

- Click in **Add a tag...** and pick one of your tags from the list. It's saved right away.
- To create a new tag, type its name and press Enter (**Press Enter to create this tag**). It
  gets the gray color and no emoji; change them later on the **My tags** page.
- Click a pill's **×** to remove that tag from the recipe. The tag itself is kept.

### Similar tags

Before creating a tag, Cocotte checks whether you already have one that looks the same: same
name with different accents or case, a plural, a typo, or one name contained in the other (for
example *pates*, *Pâtes* and *Pâtes fraîches*). If so, it shows **Similar tags already exist:**
followed by those tags:

- click one of them to put **that** tag on the recipe instead,
- or click **Create "…" anyway** if you really want a new, separate tag,
- or **Cancel**.

If you type the exact name of a tag you already have, in any case, that tag is used and no new
one is created.

## Manage your tags

Open the account menu (top right) and click **My tags**, or click **Manage my tags** on a recipe
page. The page lists all your tags, alphabetically, each with its number of recipes.

- **New tag** opens a form with a **Name**, an optional **Emoji** and a **Color** (gray, red,
  orange, yellow, green, teal, blue, purple or pink). A **Preview** shows the pill as it will
  look. As you type the name, **Similar tags already exist:** lists any close match; click one
  to close the form and highlight that existing tag in the list.
- **Edit** opens the same form to rename a tag, or change its emoji or color. The change
  shows up on every recipe that has the tag.
- The bin button deletes a tag after confirmation. It's removed from all your recipes, but the
  recipes themselves are not changed.
- Click a tag's recipe count (for example **3 recipes**) to open the recipe list filtered on
  that tag.

Two of your tags can't have the same name, in any case: you get *You already have a tag with this
name.*

## Find your tagged recipes

The recipe list and the random recipe have a **My tags** filter (shown once you have at least
one tag). Pick tags in **To try, Birthdays...**: the list shows recipes with **at least one** of
them. See [Filter the list](browsing-recipes.md#filter-the-list).

On the recipe list, your tags also show as colored pills on each recipe card.
